   ●​ POPULATION:
         ○​ Fewer Blue Crabs in 2026: Noticeably fewer blue crabs were observed at Little
             Sippewissett Marsh and Woodneck Beach in summer 2026 compared with the
             previous year. One possible explanation is the unusually cold 2025–26 Cape Cod
             winter. Because blue crabs occur near the northern edge of their historical range in
             Massachusetts, winter temperature can strongly influence their survival. Cape Cod
             also experienced significant drought during 2026, potentially altering freshwater
             input and salinity within the marsh. Year-to-year differences in larval recruitment
             and tidal conditions may have contributed as well.
